20 > 15, so Hays pays more for every ton: 3 tons → $60 vs. $45.

Compare the starts

Both y-intercepts are 0: 0 tons pays $0.

f(x) = 3x + 10 vs. a table with (0, 4), (1, 9): the table grows faster (slope 5 > 3), but f starts higher (10 > 4).
