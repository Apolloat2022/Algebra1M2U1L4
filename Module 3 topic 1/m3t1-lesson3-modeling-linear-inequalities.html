Day 1 · Getting Started · Fundraising Function · SE p. 372 · ★ Essential

Elijah sells popcorn

Each camper starts with a $25 credit. Each box sells for $3.75.

1. f(b) = 3.75b + 25

2. Analyze

Independent: boxes sold. Dependent: total sales ($).

Slope 3.75: dollars per box.

y-intercept 25: the starting credit.

Getting Started, 10–15 minutes. Ask yourself: how will you represent the $25 credit in your function?

Day 1 · Activity 3.1 · Modeling Linear Inequalities · SE pp. 373–374 · ★ Essential

Read the graph at $1600

1. Draw y = 1600 to the line, then straight down: b = 420 boxes.

A linear inequality is a non-equal comparison of two linear expressions.

2. Below the line (and on it): f(b) ≤ 1600, so b ≤ 420: ● closed circle, shade left.

3. Above the line: b > 420: ○ open circle, shade right.

Activity 3.1, 25–30 minutes. Only whole numbers of boxes make sense in context. Skills Practice Sets A and B.

Day 1 · Activity 3.1 · Questions 4–5 · SE p. 375 · ★ Essential

Open or closed? Use the graph.

4. Circles

○ open: the point is not included (<, >).

● closed: the point is included (≤, ≥).

5. How many boxes?

a. at least $925

b ≥ 240

b. less than $2050

b < 540

c. exactly $700

b = 180
Check: 3.75(240) + 25 = 925; 3.75(540) + 25 = 2050; 3.75(180) + 25 = 700.

Day 2 · Activity 3.2 · Solving Two-Step Linear Inequalities · SE p. 376 · ★ Essential

First deposit: at least $1100

To solve an inequality means to determine the values of the variable that make it true.

3.75b + 25 ≥ 1100

3.75b ≥ 1075

b ≥ 286.6…

1. How many boxes?

at least 287 boxes

He can't sell part of a box, and 286 boxes is not enough.

Worked Example: solve an inequality the same way you solve an equation. Think about how accurate the answer needs to be.

Day 2 · Activity 3.2 · Questions 2–3 · SE p. 377 · ★ Essential

Write and solve

a. Most boxes and still under $600?

3.75b + 25 < 600 → b < 153.3…

at most 153 boxes

b. At least how many boxes for $1500?

3.75b + 25 ≥ 1500 → b ≥ 393.3…

at least 394 boxes

3. The method does not change for other forms: use the properties of equality.

"At least" rounds up; "at most" rounds down.

Day 2 · Activity 3.3 · Reversing Inequality Signs · SE p. 378 · ★ Essential

Hiking down from 4800 feet at 20 feet per minute

1. h(m) = −20m + 4800

Slope −20: down 20 ft each minute. y-int 4800: the start.

x-int 240: sea level after 240 min. Domain 0 ≤ m ≤ 240.

Activity 3.3, 20–25 minutes. Ask: what are the independent and dependent quantities and their units?

Day 2 · Activity 3.3 · Questions 3–6 · SE p. 379 · ★ Essential

Below 3200 feet: why does the sign flip?

3. Graph: below 3200 ft after m > 80

−20m + 4800 < 3200

−20m < −1600

m > 80  (÷ −20 flips the sign)
h(m)m
h(m) > 3200m < 80
h(m) ≥ 3200m ≤ 80
h(m) = 3200m = 80
h(m) < 3200m > 80
h(m) ≤ 3200m ≥ 80

Multiply or divide by a negative → reverse the sign.

Stamp the learning: when you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number, the inequality sign reverses. Skills Practice Set C.

Day 3 · Activity 3.4 · Solving Other Linear Inequalities · SE p. 380 · ★ Essential

5x + 2 ≥ 3x − 10

Victoria

x-terms left: 2x + 2 ≥ −10

2x ≥ −12

x ≥ −6

Kai

x-terms right: 2 ≥ −2x − 10

12 ≥ −2x, ÷ −2 flips

−6 ≤ x

Essential Question · Answered

Do the same methods work for inequalities?

Yes: graphs (horizontal lines) and properties of equality both work.

Exception: multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number reverses the inequality sign.

Graph: ○ for < and >, ● for ≤ and ≥.

Have students answer the Essential Question on the lesson opener page.
