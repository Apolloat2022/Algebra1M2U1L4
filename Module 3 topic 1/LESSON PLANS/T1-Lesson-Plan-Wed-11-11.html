Bell work (on the board)

  • Elijah starts with a $25 credit and earns $3.75 for each box he sells. Which equation gives his total sales, y, after selling x boxes? B. y = 3.75x + 25
  • Which symbol means "at least"? H. ≥
  • Which symbol means "no more than"? D. ≤
  • What value of b makes 3.75b + 25 = 400 true? F. 100
