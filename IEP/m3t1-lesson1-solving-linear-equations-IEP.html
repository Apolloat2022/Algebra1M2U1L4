Lesson 1 · Key Vocabulary

Key vocabulary

Solution

A value for the variable that makes the equation true.

x = 2 in x + 3 = 5

Properties of equality

Do the same thing to both sides and the equation stays balanced.

add, subtract, multiply, divide

Distributive property

Multiply the number outside by every term inside.

3(x − 2) = 3x − 6

No solution

No value works. The sides simplify to a false statement.

3 = 4

Infinite solutions

Every value works. Both sides are the same.

2x + 5 = 2x + 5

Coefficient

The number in front of a variable.

the 4 in 4x

Day 1 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. Marcus has $35 and saves $15 per week. Which equation gives the amount he has saved, y, after x weeks?

Formula: y = mx + b: m = rate, b = start

y = 15x + 35
1. The start is $35, so b = 35.
2. The rate is $15 per week, so m = 15.
3. y = 15x + 35.

2. What value of x makes 3(x − 2) = 12 true?

Formula: distribute, then undo + and ×

6
1. Distribute: 3x − 6 = 12.
2. Add 6: 3x = 18.
3. Divide by 3: x = 6.

3. Is x = 2 a solution of 5x − 1 = 9?

Formula: put the number in for x

Yes, because 9 = 9
1. 5(2) − 1.
2. 10 − 1 = 9.
3. 9 = 9 is true, so yes.

4. Which expression is equivalent to 4(x + 2) − x?

Formula: distribute, then combine like terms

3x + 8
1. Distribute: 4x + 8 − x.
2. Combine 4x − x = 3x: 3x + 8.

Steps to follow

1. Distribute.

2. Combine like terms on each side.

3. Move the x-terms to one side.

4. Undo + or −.

5. Undo × or ÷.

6. Check by substituting.
