Perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals: flip the fraction and change the sign. Their product is −1.

Parallel slopes are equal.

Horizontal: y = a number, slope 0. Vertical: x = a number, slope undefined. They are always perpendicular.

Point-slope form: y − y1 = m(x − x1), then get y alone.
