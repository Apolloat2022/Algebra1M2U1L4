Day 1 · Bell Work · Review · Tue, Oct 27

Warm up: work these on your own

1. What is the reciprocal of ⅔?

Formula: reciprocal: flip the fraction

3/2
1. ⅔ has 2 on top and 3 on the bottom. Flip them: 3/2.
2. Check: ⅔ · 3/2 = 1.

2. Rotate the point (8, 5) 90° counterclockwise about the origin. Where does it go?

Formula: 90° counterclockwise: (x, y) → (−y, x)

(−5, 8)
1. x = 8 and y = 5.
2. (−y, x) = (−5, 8).

3. What is the slope of the segment from (0, 0) to (8, 5)?

Formula: m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

5/8
1. Subtract the y-values: 5 − 0 = 5.
2. Subtract the x-values: 8 − 0 = 8.
3. Divide: 5 ÷ 8 = 5/8.

4. A line passes through the points (−1, 4) and (2, −2). What is the slope of the line?

Formula: m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

−2
1. Label the points: (x₁, y₁) = (−1, 4) and (x₂, y₂) = (2, −2).
2. Subtract the y-values: −2 − 4 = −6. Subtract the x-values: 2 − (−1) = 3.
3. Divide: −6 ÷ 3 = −2.

Topic 2 Lesson 3 · Steps

How to write a perpendicular line

1. Find the slope of the given line.

2. Flip it and change the sign. That is the new slope.

3. Use the point: y − y1 = m(x − x1).

4. Get y alone.

Day 2 · Bell Work · Review · Wed, Oct 28

Warm up: work these on your own

1. Line j has slope ⅔. Line k is perpendicular to it. What is the slope of k?

Formula: perpendicular: flip and change the sign

−3/2
1. Flip ⅔: 3/2.
2. Change the sign: −3/2.
3. Check: ⅔ · (−3/2) = −1.

2. What is the slope of any horizontal line?

Formula: slope = rise ÷ run

0
1. A horizontal line is flat: it never goes up or down, so the rise is 0.
2. 0 ÷ run = 0. The slope is 0.

3. Which is the equation of the vertical line through (2, −1)?

Formula: vertical line: x = a number

x = 2
1. On a vertical line, x never changes.
2. The point has x = 2, so the line is x = 2.

4. Which equation represents a line perpendicular to y = 2x + 3 that passes through the point (4, 1)?

Formula: perpendicular: flip and change the sign · y = mx + b

y = −½x + 3
1. Slope of y = 2x + 3 is 2. Flip and change the sign: −½.
2. Put in (4, 1): 1 = −½(4) + b = −2 + b.
3. b = 3: y = −½x + 3.

General form

Check it · Step by step · ★ Essential

⊥ to y = 3x, through (3, 1)

Write the perpendicular line

Step 1. The given slope is the number with x: 3.

Step 2. 3 = {{3|1}}. Flip it to {{1|3}}, then change the sign: the new slope is −{{1|3}}.

Step 3. Put m = −{{1|3}}, x1 = 3, y1 = 1 into y − y1 = m(x − x1): y − 1 = −{{1|3}}(x − 3).

Step 4. Distribute: −{{1|3}} · x = −{{1|3}}x and −{{1|3}} · (−3) = +1. Now y − 1 = −{{1|3}}x + 1.

Step 5. Add 1 to both sides to get y alone: 1 + 1 = 2.

y = −{{1|3}}x + 2
