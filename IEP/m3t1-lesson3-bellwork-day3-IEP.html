Bell Work: Review

Warm up while attendance is taken. Answer all 4 questions, then submit to see which ones you got right.

Each question has 3 choices. Use the reminders below, and press Read aloud to hear a question.

Words: at least ≥ · at most ≤ · more than > · fewer than <.

Solve like an equation: undo + and −, then × and ÷.

Flip the sign when you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number.

Graph: ○ for < and >, ● for ≤ and ≥. Shade right for > and ≥, left for < and ≤.
