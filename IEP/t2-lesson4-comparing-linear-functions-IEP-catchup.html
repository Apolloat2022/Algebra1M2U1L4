Warm up: work these on your own

1. What is the slope of the line through (−2.5, −6) and (0, 4)? (SE p. 321)

4

2. What slope does a line parallel to y = 4x − 3 have? (SE p. 321)

4

3. What slope does a line perpendicular to y = 4x − 3 have? (SE p. 321)

−¼

4. The ratio of 9th-graders to 10th-graders is 1 : 1. What is the slope of this relationship? (SE p. 320)

1
Added for this deck; not from the textbook. Have this up as students walk in; they work silently while you take attendance (about 5 minutes). The same 4 questions are on the online Day 1 bell work. Reveal the answers one at a time. Answer key: 1. Slope = (4 − (−6)) ÷ (0 − (−2.5)) = 10 ÷ 2.5 = 4. 2. Parallel lines have equal slopes. 3. The negative reciprocal of 4 is −¼. 4. Equal numbers of each grade: y = x, slope 1.

Topic 2 Lesson 4 · Steps

How to find the slope

1. Equation y = mx + b: the slope is m.

2. Table or graph: pick two points. {{change in y|change in x}}.

3. Story: look for "each" or "per".

4. Compare: same slope → parallel. Product −1 → perpendicular.

Day 2 · Bell Work · Review of Day 1 · Wed, Nov 4

Warm up: work these on your own

1. Travis County pays $15 per ton of cardboard. How much is paid for 3 tons? (SE p. 322)

$45

2. Hays County's graph passes through (0, 0) and (1, 20). What is the payout per ton? (SE p. 322)

$20

3. J(x) = 9/5x + 32 converts °C to °F. What is J(10)? (SE p. 323)

50

4. Miguel started with $0 and had $80 after 4 months. How much did he save each month? (SE p. 324)

$20
Added for this deck; not from the textbook. Have this up as students walk in; they work silently while you take attendance (about 5 minutes). The same 4 questions are on the online Day 2 bell work. Reveal the answers one at a time. Answer key: 1. 15 · 3 = 45. 2. Slope = 20 ÷ 1 = 20 dollars per ton. 3. 9/5 · 10 + 32 = 18 + 32 = 50, so 10°C is 50°F. 4. 80 ÷ 4 = 20 dollars per month.
