Day 1 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. Slope through (1, 3) and (4, 9)?

m = {{9 − 3|4 − 1}} = {{6|3}} = 2

2. Slope and y-intercept of y = −3x + 5?

m = −3, (0, 5)

3. Line with slope 4 and y-intercept (0, −2)?

y = 4x − 2

4. Distribute: −2(x + 3)

−2x − 6
Added for this deck; not from the textbook. Have this up as students walk in; they work silently while you take attendance (about 5 minutes). These review the skills today's lesson builds on: the slope formula, slope-intercept form, and distributing a negative, which students will need when they rewrite point-slope form. Reveal the answers one at a time. Watch #4: −2 times +3 is −6. Answer key: 1. Slope through (1, 3) and (4, 9): m = (9 − 3)/(4 − 1) = 6/3 = 2. 2. Slope and y-intercept of y = −3x + 5: m = −3, y-intercept (0, 5). 3. Line with slope 4 and y-intercept (0, −2): y = 4x − 2. 4. Distribute −2(x + 3): −2x − 6.

Day 2 · Bell Work · Review of Day 1

Warm up: work these on your own

1. Point-slope form: slope −4, through (3, −1) (SE p. 204)

y + 1 = −4(x − 3)

2. Point-slope form: through (2, 5) and (4, 11) (SE p. 204)

m = 3; y − 5 = 3(x − 2)

3. Rewrite #2 in slope-intercept form. (SE p. 203)

y = 3x − 1

4. Horizontal and vertical lines through (−3, 8) (SE pp. 206–207)

y = 8; x = −3
Added for this deck; not from the textbook. Have this up as students walk in; they work silently while you take attendance (about 5 minutes). It reviews Day 1. For #1, the signs flip: the point (3, −1) gives x − 3 and y + 1. For #3, distribute and add 5: y − 5 = 3x − 6, so y = 3x − 1. For #4, horizontal keeps the y-value (y = 8) and vertical keeps the x-value (x = −3): HOY VUX. Answer key: 1. Slope −4 through (3, −1): y + 1 = −4(x − 3). 2. Through (2, 5) and (4, 11): m = (11 − 5)/(4 − 2) = 3, so y − 5 = 3(x − 2). 3. #2 in slope-intercept form: y = 3x − 1. 4. Through (−3, 8): horizontal y = 8; vertical x = −3.

Lesson 4 · Vocabulary

Words we need

Slope (m)

How steep the line is.

m = {{change in y|change in x}}

A point

A place on the line, written (x, y).

(1, 4)

Point-slope form

An equation made from the slope and one point.

y − y1 = m(x − x1)
Read each word aloud. Students copy them onto their vocabulary card.

Lesson 4 · Steps

How to write point-slope form

1. Find the slope, m.

2. Find a point, (x1, y1).

3. Put them into y − y1 = m(x − x1).

4. Check the signs: x − (−4) becomes x + 4.

Leave this slide up, or give students a printed copy, while they work.

I do · Watch me

Slope 3, point (1, 4)

Step 1: m = 3

Step 2: x1 = 1, y1 = 4

Step 3: put them in.

y − 4 = 3(x − 1)
Think aloud: orange is the slope, blue is the point. The point goes in with a minus sign.

We do · Together

Slope −8, point (3, 12)

m = ____   x1 = ____   y1 = ____

m = −8,  x1 = 3,  y1 = 12

y − ____ = ____(x − ____)

y − 12 = −8(x − 3)
Students fill in the blanks on their paper first, then reveal.

You do · Try it

Write each in point-slope form

1. Slope 5, point (2, −1)

Tip: y − (−1) = y + 1

y + 1 = 5(x − 2)

2. Slope {{2|3}}, point (−4, 5)

Tip: x − (−4) = x + 4

y − 5 = {{2|3}}(x + 4)

We do · Two points

A line through (2, 5) and (4, 11)

Step 1: slope.  m = {{11 − 5|4 − 2}} = ____

m = {{6|2}} = 3

Step 2: use the point (2, 5).

y − ____ = ____(x − ____)

y − 5 = 3(x − 2)
When there is no slope given, find it first with the slope formula.

I do · Change the form

Rewrite as y = mx + b

y − 5 = 3(x − 2)

Step 1: distribute the 3.

y − 5 = 3x − 6

Step 2: add 5 to both sides.

y = 3x − 1
Same line, two forms. Slope-intercept form shows where it crosses the y-axis: (0, −1).

You do · Try it

Rewrite as y = mx + b

y − 3 = 5(x + 2)

Step 1: distribute.  y − 3 = ____

y − 3 = 5x + 10

Step 2: add 3.  y = ____

y = 5x + 13

Lesson 4 · Horizontal and Vertical

Flat lines and straight-up lines

Horizontal

y stays the same. Slope = 0.

y = 3

Vertical

x stays the same. Slope is undefined.

x = −2
Memory trick from the lesson: HOY VUX. Horizontal, zerO slope, y = a number. Vertical, Undefined slope, x = a number.
