Day 1 · Bell Work · Review · Mon, Oct 26

Warm up: work these on your own

1. f(x) = x and g(x) = f(x) − 3. How does the graph move?

Formula: outside: + moves up, − moves down

Down 3 units
1. The −3 is outside f(x), so the graph moves up or down.
2. Minus means down: down 3 units.

2. What is the slope of g(x) = 3x − 8?

Formula: y = mx + b, slope m

3
1. Match g(x) = 3x − 8 to y = mx + b.
2. m = 3, so the slope is 3.

3. What is the x-intercept of g(x) = 3x − 8?

Formula: x-intercept: set g(x) = 0 and solve

(8/3, 0)
1. 3x − 8 = 0.
2. Add 8 to both sides: 3x = 8.
3. Divide both sides by 3: x = 8/3. The point is (8/3, 0).

4. Is h(x) = −⅔x + 5 increasing or decreasing?

Formula: slope + rises, slope − falls

Decreasing
1. The slope is −⅔, the number with x.
2. It is negative, so the line falls from left to right: decreasing.

Topic 2 Lesson 2 · Vocabulary

Words we need

Inside

Changes the input: left or right.

f(x − 3)

Outside

Changes the output: up or down.

f(x) − 3

Sign flip

Inside, minus moves right and plus moves left.

f(x + 5): left 5

Topic 2 Lesson 2 · Steps

Which way does it move?

1. Look for the number. Is it inside the parentheses with x, or outside?

2. Outside: + is up, − is down.

3. Inside: − is right, + is left (the sign flips).

4. To simplify, put the inside into the rule and distribute.

1. Simplify j(x − 2).

2. Simplify j(x) − 2.

Same graph as #1? ____

No

Check it · Step by step

j(x) = 2x − 1

1. Simplify j(x − 2)

Step 1. The change is inside, so put (x − 2) where x was: 2(x − 2) − 1.

Step 2. Distribute the 2: 2 · x = 2x and 2 · (−2) = −4. Now it is 2x − 4 − 1.

Step 3. Combine the numbers: −4 − 1 = −5.

2x − 5

2. Simplify j(x) − 2

Step 1. The change is outside, so write the whole rule, then subtract 2: 2x − 1 − 2.

Step 2. Combine the numbers: −1 − 2 = −3. That gives 2x − 3.

Step 3. Compare with #1: 2x − 5 and 2x − 3 cross the y-axis at different places.

2x − 3 · not the same graph as #1

Day 2 · Bell Work · Review · Tue, Oct 27

Warm up: work these on your own

1. f(x) = x is translated right 5 units. Which equation is the new function?

Formula: right c: g(x) = f(x − c)

g(x) = f(x − 5) = x − 5
1. Right 5 means subtract 5 inside: f(x − 5).
2. For f(x) = x: f(x − 5) = x − 5.

2. For f(x) = x, a translation right 5 gives the same graph as which other translation?

Formula: for f(x) = x only: right n = down n

Down 5 units
1. Right 5: f(x − 5) = x − 5.
2. Down 5: f(x) − 5 = x − 5.
3. Same equation, so the same graph: down 5.

3. j(x) = 2x − 1 and r(x) = j(x + 4). Simplify r(x).

Formula: put (x + 4) in for x, then distribute

r(x) = 2x + 7
1. r(x) = 2(x + 4) − 1.
2. Distribute: 2 · x + 2 · 4 = 2x + 8.
3. Combine: 2x + 8 − 1 = 2x + 7.

4. For f(x) = x, is the graph of f(2x) the same as the graph of 2f(x)?

Formula: inside: f(2x) · outside: 2f(x)

Yes, both are y = 2x
1. f(2x): put 2x in for x, so 2x.
2. 2f(x): multiply x by 2, so 2x.
3. Both are y = 2x: yes, the same graph.

Topic 1 Catch-Up · Lesson 6 · Vocabulary

Words we need

Linear function

The rate of change is always the same. The graph is a straight line.

Rate of change

Change in y ÷ change in x. It is the slope.

{{8|2}} = 4

General form

a = slope, b = y-intercept

f(x) = ax + b

Factored form

a = slope, c = zero

f(x) = a(x − c)

Zero

The x that makes f(x) = 0. The line crosses the x-axis there.

Direct variation

A line through (0, 0).

y = kx

Topic 1 Catch-Up · Lesson 6 · Steps

Is the table linear?

1. Find the change in y and the change in x between each pair of rows.

2. Divide: {{change in y|change in x}}.

3. Always the same number → linear. Different → not linear.

4. If linear, find b (the y-value when x = 0) and write f(x) = ax + b.
