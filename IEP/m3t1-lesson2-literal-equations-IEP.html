Lesson 2 · Key Vocabulary

Key vocabulary

Slope-intercept form

y = mx + b. Shows the slope m and y-intercept (0, b).

y = 2x + 3

Point-slope form

y − y₁ = m(x − x₁). Shows the slope and a point.

y − 4 = 3(x + 2)

Standard form

Ax + By = C, with integers A, B, C.

6x + 3y = 12

x-intercept

Where the line crosses the x-axis. Put 0 in for y.

(5, 0)

y-intercept

Where the line crosses the y-axis. Put 0 in for x.

(0, −2)

Literal equation

A formula with letters for measures. You can solve it for any letter.

V = Bh → h = V/B

Day 1 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. What value of x makes 2(x + 3) = x − 4 true?

Formula: distribute, then get x on one side

−10
1. Distribute: 2x + 6 = x − 4.
2. Subtract x: x + 6 = −4.
3. Subtract 6: x = −10.

2. How many solutions does 3x + 1 = 3x − 5 have?

Formula: same x-term + different numbers → no solution

No solution
1. Both sides have 3x.
2. The numbers are different: 1 and −5.
3. 1 = −5 is never true: no solution.

3. What are the slope and y-intercept of y = −2x + 7?

Formula: y = mx + b: slope m, y-intercept (0, b)

slope −2, (0, 7)
1. Match to y = mx + b.
2. m = −2: slope −2.
3. b = 7: (0, 7).

4. What is the x-intercept of y = 2x − 6?

Formula: x-intercept: put 0 in for y

(3, 0)
1. 0 = 2x − 6.
2. Add 6: 6 = 2x.
3. Divide by 2: x = 3, so (3, 0).

Day 2 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. Which equation is 4x + 2y = 10 written in slope-intercept form?

Formula: get y alone: subtract, then divide every term

y = −2x + 5
1. Subtract 4x: 2y = −4x + 10.
2. Divide every term by 2.
3. y = −2x + 5.

2. What value of x makes 3x − 7 = 11 true?

Formula: undo − with +, undo × with ÷

6
1. Add 7: 3x = 18.
2. Divide by 3: x = 6.

3. P = 2ℓ + 2w. What is ℓ when P = 30 and w = 5?

Formula: put in the numbers you know, then solve

10
1. 30 = 2ℓ + 2(5).
2. 30 = 2ℓ + 10, subtract 10: 2ℓ = 20.
3. Divide by 2: ℓ = 10.

4. C = 5/9(F − 32). What is C when F = 50?

Formula: parentheses first, then multiply

10
1. Parentheses: 50 − 32 = 18.
2. Multiply: 5/9 · 18 = 10.

Day 2 · Exit Ticket

Show what you know

1. The formula for the area of a rectangle is A = ℓw. Solve the equation for w.

Tip: Undo the multiplication.

A = ℓw. Divide both sides by ______

w = ______

w = A/ℓ

2. The formula for the volume of a cylinder is V = πr²h. Solve the equation for h.

Tip: Everything multiplied by h moves together.

V = πr²h. Divide both sides by ______

h = ______

h = V/(πr²)

3. Which equation is d = rt solved for r?

Tip: Undo × with ÷.

r = d/t · r = dt · r = t/d

r = d/t
