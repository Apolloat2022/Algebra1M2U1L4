Making Sense of Representations Practice

Pick an answer for each question. You'll see right away whether it's correct, and why. The page numbers point to your Student Edition if you want to look back.

Each question has 3 choices. Use the reminders below, and press Read aloud to hear a question.

Is it linear? Find (change in y) ÷ (change in x) for every pair. Always the same → linear.

General form: f(x) = ax + b. Factored form: f(x) = a(x − c). Distribute to switch.

How much? Put the number in for x. How many? Set f(x) equal to the amount and solve.

Round up when you need at least that much (you can't sell part of a shirt).
