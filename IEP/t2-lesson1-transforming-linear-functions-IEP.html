Transforming Linear Functions

g(x) = a · f(x) + d

I can…

✓  move a line up or down and write its equation.

✓  stretch, compress or flip a line and find its new slope.

✓  write a line parallel to another line through a point.

Day 1 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. What is the slope between (1, 2) and (4, 11)?

3

2. What is the y-intercept of y = 3x − 7?

(0, −7)

3. f(x) = 2x + 1. What is f(−3)?

−5

4. Which line is parallel to y = 3x + 1?

y = 3x − 5
Added for this deck; not from the textbook. Have this up as students walk in; they work silently while you take attendance (about 5 minutes). The same 4 questions are on the online Day 1 bell work. Reveal the answers one at a time. Answer key: 1. Slope = (11 − 2) ÷ (4 − 1) = 9 ÷ 3 = 3. 2. In y = mx + b, the y-intercept is (0, b), so it is (0, −7). 3. 2(−3) + 1 = −6 + 1 = −5. 4. Parallel lines have the same slope. Only y = 3x − 5 has slope 3.

Day 2 · Bell Work · Review of Day 1

Warm up: work these on your own

1. f(x) = x is translated up 4 units. Which equation is the new function? (SE p. 272)

g(x) = x + 4

2. The point (2, 5) is translated down 3 units. Where does it go? (SE p. 273)

(2, 2)

3. f(x) = 2x − 1. What is the slope of g(x) = f(x) + 6? (SE p. 275)

2

4. f(x) = 2x − 1. What is the y-intercept of g(x) = f(x) + 6? (SE p. 275)

(0, 5)
Added for this deck; not from the textbook. Have this up as students walk in; they work silently while you take attendance (about 5 minutes). The same 4 questions are on the online Day 2 bell work. Reveal the answers one at a time. Answer key: 1. A vertical translation adds to the output: g(x) = f(x) + 4 = x + 4. 2. A vertical translation changes only y: 5 − 3 = 2, so (2, 2). 3. A vertical translation does not change the slope. g(x) = 2x + 5 still has slope 2. 4. g(x) = 2x − 1 + 6 = 2x + 5, so the y-intercept moves from (0, −1) up 6 to (0, 5).

Day 3 · Bell Work · Review of Day 2

Warm up: work these on your own

1. f(x) = x and g(x) = 3f(x). What is the slope of g(x)? (SE p. 277)

3

2. How is g(x) = −2f(x) related to f(x) = x? (SE p. 278)

Stretched vertically by 2 and reflected across the x-axis

3. Which function is a vertical compression of f(x) = x? (SE p. 278)

g(x) = ½f(x)

4. f(x) = x and g(x) = 2f(x) − 5. What is the y-intercept of g(x)? (SE p. 280)

(0, −5)
Added for this deck; not from the textbook. Have this up as students walk in; they work silently while you take attendance (about 5 minutes). The same 4 questions are on the online Day 3 bell work. Reveal the answers one at a time. Answer key: 1. g(x) = 3x. A vertical dilation by 3 multiplies the slope of f(x) = x by 3. 2. The 2 stretches the graph and the negative sign reflects it across the x-axis. 3. Multiplying by a number between 0 and 1 compresses the graph toward the x-axis. 4. g(x) = 2x − 5. The dilation keeps (0, 0) fixed, then the graph moves down 5.

Topic 2 Lesson 1 · Steps

How to read g(x) = a · f(x) + d

1. Start with f(x) = x: slope 1, through (0, 0).

2. a is the new slope. A negative a flips the line.

3. d moves the line up (+) or down (−). The y-intercept is (0, d).

4. Plot (0, d), then count the slope a.

Leave this slide up, or give students a printed copy, while they work.

I do · Watch me

Move f(x) = x up 5

Add 5 to every output.

p(x) = f(x) + 5
p(x) = x + 5

Slope: still 1. y-intercept: (0, 5).

Think aloud: every point moves straight up 5. The lines are parallel.

We do · Together

Move j(x) = 2x − 1 down 4

r(x) = j(x) − ____

r(x) = j(x) − 4

Simplify: 2x − 1 − 4 = ____

r(x) = 2x − 5

Slope ____   y-intercept ____

slope 2, (0, −5)
Students fill in the blanks on their paper first, then reveal.

I do · Stretch

Multiply f(x) = x by 4

Multiply every output by 4.

a(x) = 4x

The slope is now 4: steeper.

With −4, the line also flips:

c(x) = −4x
All three lines pass through (0, 0): multiplying 0 gives 0.

You do · Try it

Describe the change and write g(x)

1. g(x) = 2 · f(x) + 7

stretch by 2, up 7: 2x + 7

2. g(x) = {{1|2}} · f(x) − 3

compress by {{1|2}}, down 3: {{1|2}}x − 3

We do · Parallel line

Parallel to y = −3x − 1, through (−1, 5)

Step 1: same slope.  m = ____

m = −3

Step 2: 5 = −3(−1) + b, so b = ____

b = 2

Step 3: write it.

y = −3x + 2
Parallel lines have the same slope; only b changes.

You do · Try it

Parallel to y = 2x

1. Through (0, 4)

y = 2x + 4

2. Through (2, 0)

0 = 2(2) + b

y = 2x − 4

Exit Ticket

Show what you know

1. g(x) = f(x) + 6. Up or down? How far?

up 6

2. What is the slope of g(x) = 5 · f(x)?

5

3. Write the line parallel to y = 4x + 1 through (0, −2).

y = 4x − 2
