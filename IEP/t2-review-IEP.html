Translate: f(x) + d moves the graph up (d > 0) or down (d < 0). The slope stays the same.

Dilate: a · f(x) stretches when |a| > 1, compresses when 0 < |a| < 1, and reflects across the x-axis when a < 0.

Inside the parentheses changes the input (horizontal). Outside changes the output (vertical).

f(x − c) moves right c. f(x + c) moves left c. The sign flips.

Perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals: flip the fraction and change the sign. Their product is −1.

Parallel slopes are equal.

Slope from a table or two points: (change in y) ÷ (change in x).

Same slope → parallel. Slopes multiply to −1 → perpendicular. Otherwise neither.
