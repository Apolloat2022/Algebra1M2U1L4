Lesson 3 · Key Vocabulary

Key vocabulary

Linear inequality

A comparison with <, ≤, >, or ≥ instead of =.

3.75b + 25 ≥ 1100

At least / no less than

This much or more: ≥.

at least 5 → x ≥ 5

At most / no more than

This much or less: ≤.

at most 5 → x ≤ 5

Open circle ○

The number is NOT included: < or >.

x > 2

Closed circle ●

The number IS included: ≤ or ≥.

x ≥ 2

Flip the sign

Multiply or divide by a negative → reverse the sign.

−2x < 6 → x > −3

Day 1 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. Elijah starts with a $25 credit and earns $3.75 for each box he sells. Which equation gives his total sales, y, after selling x boxes?

Formula: y = mx + b: m = rate, b = start

y = 3.75x + 25
1. Start: b = 25.
2. Rate: m = 3.75.
3. y = 3.75x + 25.

2. Which symbol means "at least"?

Formula: at least = this much or more

≥
1. "At least 5" allows 5.
2. It also allows more than 5.
3. Greater than or equal: ≥.

3. Which symbol means "no more than"?

Formula: no more than = this much or less

≤
1. "No more than 5" allows 5.
2. It also allows less than 5.
3. Less than or equal: ≤.

4. What value of b makes 3.75b + 25 = 400 true?

Formula: subtract the start, then divide by the rate

100
1. Subtract 25: 3.75b = 375.
2. Divide by 3.75: b = 100.

Day 1 · Exit Ticket

Show what you know

1. Maya has $40 and earns $12 per hour. Which equation gives the total, y, she has after working x hours?

Tip: Rate goes with x. The start is b.

y = 12x + 40 · y = 40x + 12 · y = −12x + 40

y = 12x + 40

2. 4 notebooks at $1.75, pens at $0.90. Equation for x pens?

Tip: Find the fixed cost first: 4 notebooks × $1.75.

Fixed cost: 4 × 1.75 = ______

y = ______x + ______

y = 0.90x + 7

3. Sales of at most $1150: inequality for b?

Tip: At most → ≤. ≤ gets a closed circle.

3.75b + 25 ______ 1150

b ______ ______

Circle: open / closed. Shade: left / right

b ≤ 300, closed circle at 300, shaded left

Day 2 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. What value of x makes 2x + 5 = 17 true?

Formula: undo + with −, undo × with ÷

6
1. Subtract 5: 2x = 12.
2. Divide by 2: x = 6.

2. Which describes the graph of x ≥ 2 on a number line?

Formula: ≥ or ≤: closed circle · > or <: open circle

closed circle at 2, shaded right
1. ≥ includes 2: closed circle.
2. Greater than: shade right.

3. Is x = −5 a solution of −x > 4?

Formula: put the number in for x

Yes, because 5 > 4
1. −(−5) = 5.
2. 5 > 4 is true, so yes.

4. Chloe buys 2 muffins for $1.50 each and some juices for $2.25 each. Which equation gives the total cost, y, for x juices?

Formula: y = mx + b: rate × amount + fixed cost

y = 2.25x + 3
1. Fixed cost: 2 · 1.50 = 3.
2. Rate: $2.25 per juice.
3. y = 2.25x + 3.

Tip: Subtract 5, then divide by −2 and flip.

3. Tire: 26 psi, −0.1 psi/min, stay ≥ 22. Solve for t.

Tip: At or above → ≥. Divide by −0.1 and flip.

26 − 0.1t ______ 22

Day 3 · Bell Work · Review

Warm up: work these on your own

1. What is the solution set of −4x > 12?

Formula: ÷ by a negative → flip the sign

x < −3
1. Divide by −4: 12 ÷ −4 = −3.
2. Negative divisor: flip > to <.
3. x < −3.

2. What is the solution set of 2x + 3 ≤ x − 1?

Formula: get x on one side, like an equation

x ≤ −4
1. Subtract x: x + 3 ≤ −1.
2. Subtract 3: x ≤ −4.

3. Which inequality means the same as −6 ≤ x?

Formula: switch sides → the sign points the other way

x ≥ −6
1. −6 ≤ x: −6 is less than or equal to x.
2. So x is greater than or equal to −6: x ≥ −6.

4. What value of x makes 3(x − 2) = 9 true?

Formula: distribute, then undo + and ×

5
1. Distribute: 3x − 6 = 9.
2. Add 6: 3x = 15.
3. Divide by 3: x = 5.
