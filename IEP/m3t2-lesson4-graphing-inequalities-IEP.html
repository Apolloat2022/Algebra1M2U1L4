IEP version of Lesson 4: a modified lesson that keeps the core skills (the starred activities) and cuts the number of items. Each skill follows I do, We do (sentence frames), You do. Apply each student's IEP accommodations too: extended time, read-aloud, breaks, calculator, small group.

Lesson 4 · Key Vocabulary

Key vocabulary

Inequality in two variables

Uses <, >, ≤ or ≥ with x and y.

2x + 3y ≥ 20

Boundary line

The line that splits the plane. Graph it like an equation.

y = 4x − 6

Half-plane

One side of the boundary line.

the shaded side

Solid line

For ≤ or ≥: points on the line are solutions.

y ≥ x

Dashed line

For < or >: points on the line are not solutions.

y > x

Test point

Plug in a point, often (0, 0), to pick the side.

0 > −6 true
Preview the key terms. Leave up or print as a reference card.

Lesson 4 · Steps Chart

Steps to follow

1. Graph the boundary line.

2. Solid for ≤ or ≥. Dashed for < or >.

3. Test (0, 0) in the inequality.

4. True: shade the side with (0, 0). False: shade the other side.

Anchor chart: students keep this out for every problem.

Day 1 · I do · ★ Essential · Graph an inequality

Watch: y > 4x − 6

1. Boundary y = 4x − 6: start at −6, up 4 right 1.

2. Symbol >: dashed line.

3. Test (0, 0): 0 > −6 true.

4. Shade the side with (0, 0).

dashed, shade above
I do: model every step aloud. Students copy the steps into their notes.

Day 1 · Notes to Keep

Notes to keep: Day 1

Boundary line

Graph it like y = mx + b.
≤ ≥ solid. < > dashed.

Test point

Try (0, 0). True → shade its side.
False → shade the other side.

Solutions

Every point in the shaded half-plane works.

Day 2 · I do · ★ Essential · Use the graph

Watch: Phone card: 0.2x + 0.5y ≤ 50. Is (100, 80) OK?

1. 0.2(100) + 0.5(80)

2. 20 + 40 = 60

3. 60 ≤ 50 is false.

No, it costs $60

Notes to keep: Day 2

Above or below
