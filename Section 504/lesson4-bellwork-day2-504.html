Point-slope form: y − y1 = m(x − x1). The signs flip: x − (−2) = x + 2.

Slope: m = (change in y) ÷ (change in x).

To get y = mx + b: distribute, then add or subtract to get y alone.

Horizontal: y = a number, slope 0. Vertical: x = a number, slope undefined.
